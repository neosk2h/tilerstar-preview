The flagship

KF/992 lbf

The KF is built for structural adhesive, high-modulus sealant, and cold cartridges — the material that stalls a hardware-store gun. 992 lbf (450 kg) of plunger thrust through eleven bearings, a cast aluminum cartridge cradle 3.5 mm thick, and a shock-resisting tool-steel frame. Rotating barrel, drip-free release, fully rebuildable.

About TILER STAR

We build tools for
the last two inches.

Tile, stone, and finish work happen at the end of the job, when the schedule is gone and the budget is gone and the work is the only thing anyone will ever see. That is the part of the trade TILER STAR builds for.

We make seven things and we make them without compromise: snap-off knives, replacement blades, one-piece stainless spatulas, skeleton caulk guns, a 600 ml sausage gun with the nozzles that feed it, work gloves, and a magnetic tool holder. There is no cordless line, no accessory wall, no seasonal collection.

01

Built for one trade

Twenty-seven parts against catalogs of five thousand. A setter can read this whole line in a minute and know what he needs. That is not a small thing on a Tuesday afternoon at the supply house.

02

Nothing to fail

We remove the failure points. Spatulas are cut from a single piece of stainless — no ferrule, no rivet, no handle to work loose. Blades are oil-quenched so the edge holds through a full board of backer. Guns come apart with hand tools, because a tool you can rebuild is a tool you keep.

03

Nothing to hide

We buy each part where it is made best, and we print where that is. When we don't have it in writing from the factory, we say so instead of guessing. Below is the whole ledger — including the nine SKUs we can't answer for yet.
